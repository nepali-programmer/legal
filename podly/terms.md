## What Podly is

Podly is a companion app for wireless earbuds on Android. It reads battery
levels and wear state, shows a card when you open your case, pauses playback
when you remove an earbud, and changes settings such as noise control on
earbuds paired with your phone. It is provided by Nepali Programmer ("we").
