Contact

Email support@nepaliprogrammer.com.np. Include your device model, OS version and the app version from Settings, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please do not attach a document you would not want us to read; a description of it (page count, size, where it came from) is usually enough.

Common questions

Where do my files go? Everything the app produces is saved in a folder named "CV Maker & PDF" in your device's documents area. The Files tab lists them; any file manager can reach them too. Nothing is uploaded.

Does the app need internet? Only for ads, anonymous analytics, crash reports and remote configuration. Every tool, and the CV maker, works offline.

How do I turn off analytics or crash reports? Settings → Privacy. Both toggles take effect immediately.

How do I change my ad consent? Settings → Privacy → "Privacy options" reopens Google's consent form. On iOS, tracking permission is also under the system Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

A PDF will not open. Encrypted PDFs need their password; the app asks for it. A file that is not actually a PDF (a renamed image, a corrupt download) is refused with a message. If a valid file is refused, email us the details above.

Can I move my CVs to another device? CV tab → Back up writes one file containing every CV and photo. Send it to yourself (Drive, mail, AirDrop) and use Restore on the other device. Restore never overwrites: a CV that already exists is restored as a copy.

Which languages can a CV be written in? The CV maker sets text in Latin script. Characters outside it are shown as "?" in the PDF.
