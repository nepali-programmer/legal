# PinData Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 September 2026
**Applies to:** the PinData Chrome extension, all versions from 1.0.0

## Summary

PinData saves the values you choose from web pages ("pins") and keeps them in
your own browser. It has no backend, no account, and no analytics. The only
network request PinData ever makes is a licence check for PinData Pro, and that
request never contains anything you pinned.

## What PinData stores, and where

Everything below is stored locally in your browser profile (IndexedDB and
`chrome.storage.local`) on the device where you installed PinData:

- **Pin configuration** — the name you gave the pin, the page address it came
  from, the page title, the CSS selector and attribute PinData reads, your URL
  matching mode, and any formatting rules you set.
- **Captured values and their history** — the value PinData read from the page
  each time it refreshed, with timestamps.
- **Settings** — history limit, auto-update preference, advanced mode,
  delete-confirmation preference, and whether onboarding has been shown.
- **Pro licence status** — whether this installation is paid or in a trial, the
  email address you used at checkout (if any), and when the status was last
  checked.

PinData does **not** store or read your browsing history, your keystrokes, your
cookies, your passwords, form data you did not pin, or anything from a page you
did not explicitly click to pin.

## What leaves your device

1. **Nothing you pin.** Page content, page addresses, captured values, history
   and pin configuration are never transmitted anywhere. There is no PinData
   server to send them to.
2. **Licence checks for PinData Pro.** Payments are handled by
   [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com), which uses
   [Stripe](https://stripe.com) as its payment processor. To check whether this
   installation has paid, PinData asks `https://extensionpay.com` about this
   installation's own licence. That request carries an installation identifier
   generated by ExtensionPay and, once you have paid, your email address; it
   carries no pins, no values, no page addresses and no page content. If you
   never buy or trial Pro, no personal information is involved.
   - ExtensionPay's privacy policy: <https://extensionpay.com/privacy>
   - Stripe's privacy policy: <https://stripe.com/privacy>
   - PinData never receives or stores your card details.
3. **Files you export yourself.** CSV and JSON exports are written to your
   computer by you, on your instruction.

PinData does not sell or transfer your data to third parties, does not use it
for anything unrelated to its single purpose, and never uses it for
creditworthiness or lending decisions.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission                              | Why                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `activeTab`                             | Read the page you are currently looking at, only after you click the PinData button, use the keyboard shortcut, or use the context menu, so you can pick a value and refresh pins on that page. |
| `scripting`                             | Insert PinData's element picker and value reader into the current page when you ask to pin or refresh.                                                                                          |
| `storage`                               | Keep your pins, history, settings and licence status on this device.                                                                                                                            |
| `contextMenus`                          | Add "Pin something on this page" and "Open PinData Dashboard" to the right-click menu.                                                                                                          |
| `unlimitedStorage`                      | History for many pins can exceed the default local quota. All of it stays on your device.                                                                                                       |
| Host access to `extensionpay.com`       | Required by ExtensionPay so a completed payment can be reported back to the extension. PinData uses it for nothing else.                                                                        |
| Optional access to a website you choose | Only if you turn on "update automatically when I visit this site". Requested per site, at the moment you opt in, and revocable at any time.                                                     |

## Deleting your data

- **One pin:** delete it from the dashboard; its history is deleted with it.
- **Everything:** Settings → "Delete everything" (type DELETE to confirm). This
  clears all pins, all history and all settings.
- **Uninstalling PinData** removes all locally stored data.
- **Your Pro licence** is held by ExtensionPay, not by PinData. To delete that
  record, or to ask what it holds, contact ExtensionPay
  (<https://extensionpay.com>) or email us and we will help.
