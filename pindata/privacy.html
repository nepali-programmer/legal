PinData Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026 · applies to all versions from 1.0.0

Summary

PinData saves the values you choose from web pages ("pins") and keeps them in your own browser. It has no backend, no account, and no analytics. The only network request PinData ever makes is a licence check for PinData Pro, and that request never contains anything you pinned.

What PinData stores, and where

All of the following is stored locally in your browser profile (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local) on the device where you installed PinData:

  • Pin configuration — the name you gave the pin, the page address it came from, the page title, the CSS selector and attribute PinData reads, your URL matching mode, and any formatting rules you set.
  • Captured values and their history — the value PinData read from the page each time it refreshed, with timestamps.
  • Settings — history limit and your display and confirmation preferences.
  • Pro licence status — whether this installation is paid or in a trial, the email address you used at checkout (if any), and when the status was last checked.

PinData does not store or read your browsing history, your keystrokes, your cookies, your passwords, form data you did not pin, or anything from a page you did not explicitly click to pin.

What leaves your device

  1. Nothing you pin. Page content, page addresses, captured values, history and pin configuration are never transmitted anywhere. There is no PinData server to send them to.
  2. Licence checks for PinData Pro. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe as its payment processor. To check whether this installation has paid, PinData asks https://extensionpay.com about this installation's own licence. That request carries an installation identifier generated by ExtensionPay and, once you have paid, your email address; it carries no pins, no values, no page addresses and no page content. PinData never receives or stores your card details. See ExtensionPay's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy.
  3. Files you export yourself. CSV and JSON exports are written to your computer by you, on your instruction.

PinData does not sell or transfer your data to third parties, does not use it for anything unrelated to its single purpose, and never uses it for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

Permissions and why they are needed

Permission Why
activeTab Read the page you are currently looking at, only after you click the PinData button, use the keyboard shortcut, or use the context menu.
scripting Insert PinData's element picker and value reader when you ask to pin or refresh.
storage Keep your pins, history, settings and licence status on this device.
contextMenus Add PinData's two right-click menu items.
unlimitedStorage History for many pins can exceed the default local quota. All of it stays on your device.
Host access to extensionpay.com Required by ExtensionPay so a completed payment can be reported back to the extension. Used for nothing else.
Optional access to a website you choose Only if you turn on "update automatically when I visit this site". Requested per site, at the moment you opt in, and revocable at any time.

Deleting your data

  • One pin: delete it in the dashboard; its history goes with it.
  • Everything: Settings → "Delete everything" (type DELETE to confirm).
  • Uninstalling PinData removes all locally stored data.
  • Your Pro licence is held by ExtensionPay, not by PinData. Contact ExtensionPay, or email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published here with a new effective date and noted in the extension's changelog before the release that introduces them.
